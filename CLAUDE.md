# Notes for working on this portfolio

## Writing style (Kevin's rule: no AI tone)

All text on the site should read as if Kevin wrote it himself. Avoid anything that sounds AI-generated:

- No cute or chatty asides, such as "It plays, so go ahead", "Weighing your options?" or "read it".
- No rhetorical questions as hooks, such as "Can windowing fix that?"
- No slogans, aphorisms or punchy contrasts, such as "No window wins on both" or "the real ceiling".
- No instructions to the reader, such as "Pick a fake" or "drag it".
- No filler labels, such as "Live", "The full story" or "What's in it".
- No em dashes, no lists of three for rhythm, no buzzwords.
- Use plain, factual sentences in British English. Say what something is and what Kevin did.
